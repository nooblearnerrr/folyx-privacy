FOLYX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

FOLYX is a free PDF editor for Windows, published by nasrulameen. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use FOLYX.

The short version

FOLYX works offline. Your PDFs, signatures and edits stay on your computer. FOLYX has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. The only time anything leaves your computer is when you use an AI feature in the CV Builder.

Files and data stored on your computer

  • The PDFs you open and save stay wherever you keep them. FOLYX does not upload them anywhere.
  • Settings, recent-file history, saved signatures and error logs are stored only on your computer, in your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\FOLYX).
  • If you enter an API key for an AI provider, it is stored encrypted with Windows data protection so that only your Windows account can read it.

Uninstalling FOLYX removes this data.

AI features (CV Builder only)

The CV Builder can use AI to help write or improve your CV. This is optional and only runs when you press an AI button. When you do, the text needed for that request (for example the CV details you typed) is sent to the AI service you have chosen:

  • FOLYX Free AI (the default) sends the request to Pollinations (text.pollinations.ai), a third-party AI service. No key or account is needed.
  • Other providers you choose yourself (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI or a custom one) receive the request under that provider's own privacy policy, using your own key.
  • Ollama runs on your own computer, and nothing leaves it.

Please do not put information you consider sensitive into AI requests. If the AI service is unavailable, FOLYX falls back to a built-in offline writer and nothing is sent.

What FOLYX does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not include analytics, telemetry or advertising.

Microsoft Store

If you install FOLYX from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect data about installs and crashes under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer receives only anonymous, aggregate reports from Microsoft.

Children

FOLYX does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support contact on the FOLYX Microsoft Store page.
